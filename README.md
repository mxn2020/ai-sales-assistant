# AI Sales Assistant (Lead Qualification & Follow-Up)

## Pages & Features

### 1. Dashboard
- Pipeline value & conversion rate overview
- Hot leads & at-risk deals alerts
- AI-scored lead quality distribution
- Follow-up activity feed
- Revenue forecast widget

### 2. Lead Scoring
- AI auto-scoring based on behavior & profile data
- Scoring criteria configuration (engagement, demographics, fit)
- Score history & trend per lead
- Bulk re-score triggers

### 3. Lead Qualification
- AI chatbot for initial lead qualification
- Custom qualification questionnaire builder
- Auto-tag leads (hot, warm, cold)
- Disqualification reasons tracking
- MQL → SQL conversion funnel

### 4. Smart Follow-Up
- AI-suggested next actions per lead
- Auto-generated follow-up emails
- Optimal send time recommendation
- Follow-up sequence builder (drip campaigns)
- Reminder & escalation alerts

### 5. Conversation Intelligence
- AI call & email analysis per deal
- Key objection & buying signal detection
- Competitor mention tracking
- Talk-to-listen ratio analysis

### 6. Pipeline View
- Kanban board with AI deal risk indicators
- Deal health scoring
- Predicted close date & probability
- Stale deal alerts & win/loss analysis

### 7. Reports
- Lead source performance
- Conversion funnel analysis
- Rep activity & effectiveness metrics
- AI accuracy & recommendation impact
- Export as PDF / CSV

### 8. Settings
- Lead scoring model configuration
- Follow-up sequence defaults
- CRM integration (Salesforce, HubSpot, etc.)
- Email & calendar sync
- Team access & permissions
